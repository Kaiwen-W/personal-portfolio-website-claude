Build Circle is less a piece of software and more a piece of community design — though it has a small site holding it together.

## Why start it

University is full of people quietly building things and almost no structured way for them to find each other.

## How it works

- Fortnightly demo nights where everyone shows progress
- An end-of-year showcase open to the whole department
- A members directory and a feed of current work

## What I learned

The hardest part of a community is not starting it — it is the unglamorous work of keeping a rhythm.
