Gesture Fan is a fan that pays attention. It is the project that taught me the most about working at the seam between hardware and software.

## The concept

A normal fan keeps pointing wherever it pointed last. Gesture Fan watches the room with a small camera and aims itself at people instead.

## Building it

- It finds the nearest person and rotates to follow them
- It reads a few hand gestures so you can change speed without a remote
- A companion app exposes finer control over the local network

## The honest part

It took far longer than expected — physical things fail in physical ways. But a fan that turns to look at you is a small piece of magic.
