People ask what I use often enough that a list felt easier than repeating myself. This is a living page — I will update it as things change.

## Editor and terminal

I write almost everything in a single editor with a minimal theme and as few plugins as I can get away with. The terminal does the heavy lifting; I am happiest with a keyboard and a prompt.

## Languages

Day to day it is mostly **TypeScript** and **Python**. I reach for Python when a problem is mostly thinking, and TypeScript when a problem is mostly interface.

## Hardware

- A laptop that is more portable than powerful
- A mechanical keyboard with quiet switches
- An e-reader, because screens are tiring

## Principles over tools

The specific tools matter less than the habits. Keep the toolchain small, learn it deeply, and do not chase every new thing. The best setup is the one you stop thinking about.
